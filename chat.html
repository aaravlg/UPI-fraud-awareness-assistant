Know the Warning Signs

Three common tricks to watch out for

📱

Fake QR Codes

Be careful. Scanning a QR code to receive money is usually unnecessary. Check before you scan.

🔐

OTP Scams

Never share your OTP, UPI PIN or banking credentials with anyone, even if they claim to be from your bank.

⚠️

Collect Requests

Receiving money should not require you to enter your UPI PIN. Verify every payment request carefully.
